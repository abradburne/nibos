The console

Select + Start leaves DSlot for the console: a shell, on the top screen, with a keyboard on the
touch screen.

- Touch the keys to type. Shift, Ctrl, Alt and Fn are tapped before a key (twice to keep them
  on).
- The buttons type too: the D-pad the arrows, A Enter, B Backspace, X Tab, Y Ctrl-C, L Shift
  and R Ctrl while held.
- Menu + X hides the keyboard and shows it again.
- A USB keyboard in the host-only USB-C port, or a Bluetooth one, works as well.
- nib help lists the commands this device has.

From a computer, ssh nibos@nibos.local with the key from provision.toml's [ssh].

Restart (hold power) to come back to DSlot.
