Sleep and charging

# Sleep
- Close the lid, or press power: sleep. Open it, or press power: wake. Wi-Fi reconnects about
  2 seconds later.
- Asleep on battery, the device powers off after an hour. System > Power > Power off when
  asleep changes that.

# Charging
- The light is green while the device runs, and amber while the charger is plugged in (asleep
  too).
- Plugged into a device that is switched off, the charger switches it on and it charges
  asleep: dark, with the amber light on. Press power to use it. Unplugging it switches it off
  again.
- Battery protect (System > Power) stops charging at about 80%.
- Charge from the USB-C port that charges the device. Keyboards and pads go in the other
  USB-C port.
