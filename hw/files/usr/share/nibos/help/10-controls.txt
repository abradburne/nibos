Controls

# On the shelf
- D-pad left and right, or a swipe: browse the games.
- A, or drag the cart down: play the game, or open a folder of games.
- Select + A: play the game from the start, not from where you left off.
- B: out of the folder of games open.
- X: the game's page. Y: mark it as a favourite, or not.
- L and R, or a tap on a tab: the page before or after.
- Select: the system picker. Start: how the page is shown.
- Back (beside Menu): this menu.
- Select + Start: leave DSlot for the console.

# In a game
- Menu: the game's pause menu.
- The volume keys: the volume.
- Menu + the volume keys: both screens' brightness.

# Power
- Close the lid, or press power: sleep. Open it, or press power: wake.
- Hold power for a second: the power menu (Sleep, Restart, Power off). In a game, the game
  is saved and put away first.
- Hold power for about 10 seconds: the power is cut, without saving anything. Only for a
  device that doesn't answer.
