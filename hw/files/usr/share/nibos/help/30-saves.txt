Saves

Each game's saves and save states are kept in Saves/<SYSTEM>/<ROM name>/ on the NIBOS-DATA
drive, the first card's games drive.

When you quit a game, its emulator saves where you left off: its resume point, shown over the
game's box on the top screen. The game's pause menu has numbered save states as well.

To start a game from the beginning once, hold Select and press A: "Starting fresh" shows as
the cart goes in. The resume point isn't deleted: it is renamed with .before-fresh at the end,
in the game's saves folder, so renaming it back brings it back.

On the menu's Game page, clearing the resume state and deleting the saves each ask first: A,
then A again.

An update keeps your games, saves and settings. Writing a new image keeps NIBOS-DATA, and puts
the settings back from the copy nibOS keeps in its nibos/settings/ folder. The
RetroAchievements sign-in isn't copied: sign in again after a new image.
