The menu

Back (beside Menu) opens this menu on the touch screen. It has a page for each level of
settings: Game (the game picked on the shelf), its console (DS, GBA...) and System, with
Network and Achievements beside them.

- L and R, or a tap on a tab: the page before or after.
- L2 and R2, or a tap on a section: the next part of a long page.
- D-pad up and down: pick a row.
- Left and right, A, or a tap on the row: change it.
- Y: the row goes back to what it inherits.
- B or Back: close the menu.

A setting for a game wins over its console's, which wins over the system's, and each row says
where its value comes from.

# Reading a page like this one
- Up and down: a line. Left and right: a screen.
- Drag it with a finger.
- B: back to the list.
